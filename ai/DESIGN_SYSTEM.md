# CoralOps Design System

## Theme

Enterprise AI Infrastructure

## Colors

Background: zinc-950

Panel: zinc-900

Border: zinc-800

Primary: blue-500

Success: emerald-500

Danger: red-500

Warning: amber-500

## Typography

Font: Inter

Heading Rules:

- Avoid giant hero headings

- Operational density preferred

- Medium-weight hierarchy

## Border Radius

- rounded-lg

- rounded-xl maximum

## Shadows

- subtle only

- avoid glowing effects

## Motion

Allowed:

- fades

- subtle reveals

- hover transitions

- timeline updates

- loading shimmer

Forbidden:

- parallax

- floating animations

- exaggerated transforms

- gaming effects

## Layout Rules

- Left operational sidebar

- Top observability bar

- Reusable shell layouts

- Consistent panel spacing

## Forbidden Aesthetics

- Glassmorphism

- Neon cyberpunk

- Giant gradients

- Oversized border radius
